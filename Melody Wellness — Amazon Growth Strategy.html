05Subscribe & Save Strategy

90+ Day Consistency Framework

One bottle provides a 60-day supply. Melody is well positioned to introduce a '90+ day minimum' consistency framework across:

  • Listing messaging
  • Premium A+
  • Inserts
  • Advertising

Recommended messaging:

  • "Consistency is key"
  • "Commit to a minimum of 90+ days"
  • "Small daily actions compound over time"
Coupon Structure Refinement

The current 25% Subscribe & Save coupon supports strong conversion and should remain active short term. Long term, test:

  • Stronger ongoing S&S value
  • Smoother first-to-second order transition
  • Reduced aggressive first-order discounting

08Creative Strategy

The creative foundation is strong — scientific support is clear, brand identity is consistent and more developed than Amazon competitors. Priority is refreshing content, not a redesign: adding warmth, elevating the lifestyle layer, and creating genuine emotional connection.

The objective is to pull back slightly from the clinical/functional element — separating Melody from competitors — and lean into supplements as a life partner, driving conversion and retaining customer loyalty.

Strategic Creative Changes
Warmth & Emotional Connection
  • Show a range of ages through lifestyle imagery
    • 35–45 proactive age range: active people, younger families — alluding to longevity and prevention
    • 45–70 age range: gentler activity, relaxed feel, imagery with grandchildren
  • Communicate the emotional payoff of showing up for your health — more time and presence with loved ones
Premium Positioning Through Imagery
  • Natural materials cohesive with the amber glass bottle
  • Model clothes and locations reinforce premium feel
  • Carry brand colours throughout all lifestyle imagery for cohesion
  • Incorporate neutral tones to elevate and visually communicate simplicity
Routine & Simplicity
  • Morning routine moment — help consumers visualise incorporating Melody into daily life
  • Demonstrate ease of consistent use and 4-in-1 cardiovascular support
  • Maintain clean ingredient imagery to reinforce science behind the supplements
  • Clean up graphics for a more premium, cohesive look
Competitor Landscape

Amazon competitors lean heavily into a clinical feel that lacks emotional connection for brand loyalty. Imagery is inconsistent — damaging trust and interrupting the buying experience.

09Advertising Strategy

Most of the ad budget should be directed at search terms and targets where Melody's listing converts. CVR data at the search term and target level is the primary lens for all targeting, bidding, and budget decisions.

Where We Are Today

The account is live across Sponsored Products (SP), Sponsored Brands Video (SBV), and Sponsored Display (SD) — the right ad-type mix for a supplement brand at this stage.

SP Average CVR5.1% — but this average masks two very different stories.
High PerformersSelect search terms converting at 13–22% CVR.
Key OpportunityEnsure every campaign is directed at terms where purchase intent is high and the listing is proven to convert.
Our Approach — Three Phases
1
Optimise for Conversion
Weeks 1–2
  • Review all search term and product target CVR; reduce or remove targets below 2% CVR threshold
  • Scale budget on proven high-CVR terms: 'lower blood pressure' (22%), 'natural blood pressure supplements' (20%), 'pills to lower high pressure' (13%)
  • Reduce Top of Search placement multipliers on campaigns where search term CVR is below account average
  • Weekly negation pass on Auto and Broad campaigns: any search term with 3+ clicks and zero conversions is excluded
  • Consolidate overlapping campaigns targeting the same keywords
2
Restructure & Build Visibility
Weeks 3–6
  • Build dedicated single-keyword Exact campaigns for the top 10 priority terms
  • Launch a dedicated ranking campaign for 'blood pressure supplements' — currently ranked outside the top 60
  • Increase Sponsored Display repurchase campaign budget — performing at the strongest ACOS in the account
  • Begin testing Sponsored Brands headline ads with Subscribe & Save messaging
3
Full Funnel & Subscribe & Save Growth
Month 2–3+
  • Launch S&S-focused Sponsored Display campaigns targeting customers who purchased once but haven't yet subscribed
  • Expand Sponsored Brands Video into nattokinase and cardiovascular-specific keyword clusters
  • Test AMC (Amazon Marketing Cloud) audiences for retargeting listing visitors
  • Align ad creative with refreshed lifestyle imagery once new assets are live
Keyword Priority Tiers
Tier 1 — Scale Now
Terms already converting profitably at high CVR — highest bids and most budget.
  • 'lower blood pressure' — 22% CVR
  • 'natural blood pressure supplements' — 20% CVR
  • 'pills to lower high pressure' — 13% CVR
Tier 2 — Build & Rank
High-volume terms with lower CVR today. Sustained investment needed to build rank over time.
  • 'blood pressure supplements'
  • 'high blood pressure supplements'
Tier 3 — Niche Differentiation
Terms aligned to unique ingredients — harder for generic competitors to compete on.
  • 'nattokinase blood pressure supplement' (already converting)
  • 'nattokinase supplement'
  • 'cardiovascular supplement'
How Advertising Supports the Broader Strategy
  • Subscribe & Save growth: Phase 3 Sponsored Display and Brand Tailored Promotion campaigns convert one-time buyers into subscribers, directly supporting the 90-day consistency framework.
  • Premium positioning: concentrating spend on high-CVR, high-intent search terms improves traffic quality, protecting the premium price point.
  • Nattokinase differentiation: Tier 3 keyword expansion ensures Melody shows up when shoppers search specifically for nattokinase.
  • Creator Connections alignment: as Creator Connections traffic grows, Sponsored Display retargeting will capture and convert that warm audience more efficiently.
