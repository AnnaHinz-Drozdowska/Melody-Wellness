Amazon Growth Strategy

MELODY WELLNESS

Premium Preventive Cardiovascular Wellness

Prepared by BAD Marketing  ·  Amazon Department

01Long-Term Objectives
# 1
Become a Top Cardiovascular Wellness Brand on Amazon

Expand Melody from a single-product brand into a recognised leader within the cardiovascular wellness category, supported by strong organic visibility, customer loyalty, and future product line expansion.

# 2
Build the Category's Strongest Subscribe & Save Customer Base

Establish a large, highly engaged base of recurring customers by positioning Melody as a long-term daily wellness routine rather than a short-term supplement purchase.

# 3
Become the Most Trusted Cardiovascular Wellness Brand on Amazon

Build a reputation for science-backed formulations, education, transparency, and long-term customer loyalty — making Melody the go-to brand for proactive cardiovascular wellness.

02Current Performance
CVRImproved significantly over time, peaking at 16% — very strong for a premium-priced supplement.
Growth OpportunityStronger conversion and retention signals than traffic acquisition. The primary growth opportunity is traffic volume and click share.
Subscribe & SaveAlready healthy penetration for an early-stage brand — confirms strong repeat-use and daily habit potential.
RetentionCustomers who remain beyond the first delivery show strong LTV, making subscription growth a major long-term opportunity.
AssetsStrategically strong foundation supporting premium positioning, trust, and retention psychology. Opportunity is refinement — not a full rebuild.

Melody's strongest long-term opportunity: positioning as a premium preventive cardiovascular wellness brand built around consistency, trust, and proactive care.

03Strategic Focus
1. Position Melody as a Premium Proactive Cardiovascular Wellness Brand

Move beyond 'premium nattokinase supplement' positioning. Strengthen:

  • Complete cardiovascular support
  • Prevention & long-term wellness
  • Consistency and daily proactive care
  • Complete cardiovascular wellness through simple daily consistency
2. Improve SERP Competitiveness and Click Share

The account shows healthy conversion and retention foundations but weaker CTR and click share competitiveness. The goal is to sharpen differentiation and positioning directly in search results.

3. Lean Aggressively into Subscribe & Save Growth

Melody is a strong candidate for long-term retention, daily habit formation, and wellness subscription.

Subscribe & Save should become a core growth pillar for the brand — not just an auto-delivery discount.

04Key Strategic Insights
Performance Signals

Current performance confirms:

  • Improving CVR over time
  • Stable premium pricing
  • Healthy Subscribe & Save penetration
Primary Opportunity

Grow click share and acquisition scale through:

  • Stronger SERP differentiation
  • Clearer positioning hierarchy
  • More aggressive top-of-funnel visibility
Strongest Positioning

The 4-in-1 positioning is strategically strong and should remain the focus across all listing assets, advertising, and creative.

'Most heart supplements only support one aspect of cardiovascular wellness.' — This is Melody's sharpest differentiator.

05Listing & Creative Refinements

The current creative foundation is strong and does not require a full redesign. The listing already communicates premium positioning, trust, quality, physician credibility, and system-based formulation.

Strengthen Complete Cardiovascular Support Hierarchy

Prioritise this order:

  • Complete cardiovascular support  (1st)
  • 4-in-1 system  (2nd)
  • Prevention & consistency  (3rd)
  • Ingredients & science  (4th)
Move Beyond Single-Ingredient Positioning

The current SERP presentation over-emphasises "2400 FU nattokinase." Test full-spectrum cardiovascular care messaging in the main image.

Elevate Consistency & Habit Messaging

The listing underutilises daily habit formation, long-term consistency, and routine psychology. Recommended messaging themes:

  • "Built for long-term support"
  • "Consistency matters"
  • "Designed for proactive cardiovascular wellness"
Refine 25% vs. 100% Messaging

The concept is strategically strong. Sharpen execution to elevate tone and position Melody as the premium alternative.

06Subscribe & Save Strategy

Subscribe & Save should become one of Melody's primary long-term growth engines — aligned with daily habit formation, prevention, consistency, and long-term wellness behaviour.

90+ Day Consistency Framework

One bottle provides a 60-day supply. Melody is well positioned to introduce a '90+ day minimum' consistency framework across:

  • Listing messaging
  • Premium A+
  • Inserts
  • Advertising

Recommended messaging:

  • "Consistency is key"
  • "Commit to a minimum of 90+ days"
  • "Small daily actions compound over time"
Coupon Structure Refinement

The current 25% Subscribe & Save coupon supports strong conversion and should remain active short term. Long term, test:

  • Stronger ongoing S&S value
  • Smoother first-to-second order transition
  • Reduced aggressive first-order discounting
07Product Insert Strategy

Melody is a strong candidate for a post-purchase insert focused on reinforcing long-term consistency, increasing Subscribe & Save adoption, and driving compliant review generation.

Core messaging:

  • "Consistency is key"
  • "Support your heart daily"
  • "Stay on track with Subscribe & Save"
08Creator Connections Strategy

Creator Connections should be used as an engine to drive qualified traffic, trust, and Subscribe & Save growth. The approach runs across two phases, aligned with the broader listing and advertising strategy.

Phase 1 — Activate Existing Campaigns

Two campaigns are currently live, with one showing creator interest. Due to the agency transition these have not been actively managed. Phase 1 focuses on activating that existing interest and generating content while listing refinements and PPC cleanup are underway.

Phase 2 — Launch a New Aligned Campaign

Once listing refinements, main image optimisation, and PPC structure are complete, a new campaign launches — built from scratch and fully aligned with Melody's brand positioning, Subscribe & Save growth objective, and 90+ day consistency messaging.

Recommended Creator Angles
  • Proactive heart wellness content
  • Daily wellness routines and habit formation
  • Prevention-focused lifestyle content
  • Education around the 4-in-1 cardiovascular support framework

The goal: position Melody as a long-term cardiovascular wellness habit — not a transactional supplement purchase.

09Creative Strategy

The creative foundation is strong — scientific support is clear, brand identity is consistent and more developed than Amazon competitors. Priority is refreshing content, not a redesign: adding warmth, elevating the lifestyle layer, and creating genuine emotional connection.

The objective is to pull back slightly from the clinical/functional element — separating Melody from competitors — and lean into supplements as a life partner, driving conversion and retaining customer loyalty.

Strategic Creative Changes
Warmth & Emotional Connection
  • Show a range of ages through lifestyle imagery
    • 35–45 proactive age range: active people, younger families — alluding to longevity and prevention
    • 45–70 age range: gentler activity, relaxed feel, imagery with grandchildren
  • Communicate the emotional payoff of showing up for your health — more time and presence with loved ones
Premium Positioning Through Imagery
  • Natural materials cohesive with the amber glass bottle
  • Model clothes and locations reinforce premium feel
  • Carry brand colours throughout all lifestyle imagery for cohesion
  • Incorporate neutral tones to elevate and visually communicate simplicity
Routine & Simplicity
  • Morning routine moment — help consumers visualise incorporating Melody into daily life
  • Demonstrate ease of consistent use and 4-in-1 cardiovascular support
  • Maintain clean ingredient imagery to reinforce science behind the supplements
  • Clean up graphics for a more premium, cohesive look
Competitor Landscape

Amazon competitors lean heavily into a clinical feel that lacks emotional connection for brand loyalty. Imagery is inconsistent — damaging trust and interrupting the buying experience.

10Advertising Strategy

Most of the ad budget should be directed at search terms and targets where Melody's listing converts. CVR data at the search term and target level is the primary lens for all targeting, bidding, and budget decisions.

Where We Are Today

The account is live across Sponsored Products (SP), Sponsored Brands Video (SBV), and Sponsored Display (SD) — the right ad-type mix for a supplement brand at this stage.

SP Average CVR5.1% — but this average masks two very different stories.
High PerformersSelect search terms converting at 13–22% CVR.
Key OpportunityEnsure every campaign is directed at terms where purchase intent is high and the listing is proven to convert.
Our Approach — Three Phases
1
Optimise for Conversion
Weeks 1–2
  • Review all search term and product target CVR; reduce or remove targets below 2% CVR threshold
  • Scale budget on proven high-CVR terms: 'lower blood pressure' (22%), 'natural blood pressure supplements' (20%), 'pills to lower high pressure' (13%)
  • Reduce Top of Search placement multipliers on campaigns where search term CVR is below account average
  • Weekly negation pass on Auto and Broad campaigns: any search term with 3+ clicks and zero conversions is excluded
  • Consolidate overlapping campaigns targeting the same keywords
2
Restructure & Build Visibility
Weeks 3–6
  • Build dedicated single-keyword Exact campaigns for the top 10 priority terms
  • Launch a dedicated ranking campaign for 'blood pressure supplements' — currently ranked outside the top 60
  • Increase Sponsored Display repurchase campaign budget — performing at the strongest ACOS in the account
  • Begin testing Sponsored Brands headline ads with Subscribe & Save messaging
3
Full Funnel & Subscribe & Save Growth
Month 2–3+
  • Launch S&S-focused Sponsored Display campaigns targeting customers who purchased once but haven't yet subscribed
  • Expand Sponsored Brands Video into nattokinase and cardiovascular-specific keyword clusters
  • Test AMC (Amazon Marketing Cloud) audiences for retargeting listing visitors
  • Align ad creative with refreshed lifestyle imagery once new assets are live
Keyword Priority Tiers
Tier 1 — Scale Now
Terms already converting profitably at high CVR — highest bids and most budget.
  • 'lower blood pressure' — 22% CVR
  • 'natural blood pressure supplements' — 20% CVR
  • 'pills to lower high pressure' — 13% CVR
Tier 2 — Build & Rank
High-volume terms with lower CVR today. Sustained investment needed to build rank over time.
  • 'blood pressure supplements'
  • 'high blood pressure supplements'
Tier 3 — Niche Differentiation
Terms aligned to unique ingredients — harder for generic competitors to compete on.
  • 'nattokinase blood pressure supplement' (already converting)
  • 'nattokinase supplement'
  • 'cardiovascular supplement'
How Advertising Supports the Broader Strategy
  • Subscribe & Save growth: Phase 3 Sponsored Display and Brand Tailored Promotion campaigns convert one-time buyers into subscribers, directly supporting the 90-day consistency framework.
  • Premium positioning: concentrating spend on high-CVR, high-intent search terms improves traffic quality, protecting the premium price point.
  • Nattokinase differentiation: Tier 3 keyword expansion ensures Melody shows up when shoppers search specifically for nattokinase.
  • Creator Connections alignment: as Creator Connections traffic grows, Sponsored Display retargeting will capture and convert that warm audience more efficiently.
